## Remaining Proof Before Goal Completion

Do not mark the active guided Stock Inbound goal complete until all of these are captured with screenshots/video and database row evidence where noted:

1. Logged-in Stock user completes a supplier-barcode session on `/stock/inbound`: screenshot setup, scanner, and summary; database shows matching `stock_units`, `stock_movements`, `barcode_scan_logs`, and `audit_logs`.
2. First supplier barcode teaches a rule and second same-format barcode auto-extracts weight: screenshot rule page and second scan; database shows `barcode_weight_rules.barcode_length`, weight position, digit length, decimals, and sample barcode.
3. Different-length barcode shows the length warning without silently saving wrong weight: screenshot warning; database shows no incorrect extra `stock_units` row and a failed scan/issue row when available.
4. Duplicate barcode is blocked: screenshot red duplicate warning; database shows no second `stock_units` row and the failed scan/issue trail.
5. Phone camera scan opens, scans continuously, and stops stream when closed: phone video or screenshots showing permission, scan success frame, second scan readiness, and camera indicator off after Close.
6. Handheld/Bluetooth scanner Enter input works in both inline input and scanner popup input: short video or screenshots showing the submitted barcode value and saved result.
7. Internal-label session generates a numeric label, prints or exports PDF, scans the printed label, and returns focus to next weight: label PDF/photo plus database `stock_units`, movement, scan log, and generated barcode evidence.
8. Undo Last Scan voids one current-session stock unit: screenshot before/after; database shows unit `VOIDED`, `INBOUND_VOID` movement, scan log, and audit log.
9. Manager/admin/director whole-session undo/delete voids eligible session stock: screenshot confirmation and result; database shows all eligible units voided plus `INBOUND_VOID` movement/log/audit rows.
10. Session summary is readable at about 390px width: screenshot showing product, manufacturer, total count, total weight, saved scans, failed scans, scanned-by names, Print Labels PDF, Print Session Summary, Finish Session, and manager-approved Delete Whole Session without horizontal scrolling.
